Our Story 🏠

The Happy Print Haus started in Sta. Rosa, Laguna — not as a big business plan, but as a hobby our owner simply couldn't put down. One design led to another, one print turned into an order, and before long friends, neighbors and businesses were knocking on our door.

Today we're a small, BIR-registered print studio that still treats every order like a personal project. Every shirt, mug and 3D piece that leaves our haus carries our mark of happiness — and we hope it makes you smile, too.
